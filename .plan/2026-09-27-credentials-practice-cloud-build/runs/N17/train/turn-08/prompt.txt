Good, the measured output settles both. I took your questions to the owners. Their answers, verbatim where I have them:

PM
- Claims (your PM 1): only what the scanner's request asks for. Disclose exactly the claims its query names and nothing else. No confirmation step in this version.
- Single-use (PM 2): no, not in v1. The README says it but it isn't the design: a member shows the same credential every visit. What stops replay is the per-request nonce, so the same presentation can't be reused, but the credential can be presented again to a fresh request. The wallet must not delete or "consume" the credential after presenting it.
- Storage (PM 3): out of scope for this feature.
- Format (PM 4): the wallet holds what our backend issuer issues. The app README's JWT-VC line is out of date.
- Can't satisfy (PM 5): if the credential has expired, refuse: don't build a presentation of an expired credential; raise an error to the caller (a WalletException) instead of handing over something the door will turn down. Revoked: the wallet doesn't check that; revocation is the verifier's job, it reads the status list on its side. A requested claim the credential doesn't hold: refuse with a WalletException too.
- I also asked the PM for the minimal version and what's deliberately out. Answer: ship WalletService.createPresentation(credentialId, request) returning the presentation as a JSON map: the requested subset, holder-signed, bound to nonce and domain, refusing an expired credential. Deferred on purpose: posting to response_uri and the door UI; choosing between several matching credentials and DCQL credential_sets; a not-yet-valid check in the wallet; holder-side revocation checks; enforced single-use; holder key rotation.

Verifier team
- Envelope (Verifier 1): createPresentation returns the presentation object that verify_presentation takes. Any envelope is the scanner's side, not this feature.
- Domain (Verifier 2): the requester's client_id, i.e. PresentationRequest.domain. Nonce and domain both go inside the holder-signed part; we reject a presentation whose nonce or domain isn't the one we issued.
- Naming a claim (Verifier 3): yes, the claim is the last element of the query's path; send that claim's disclosure exactly as the issuer handed it over ({salt, name, value}, unchanged), next to the unchanged credential.
- Proof type and purpose (Verifier 4): sign with Ed25519Signature2020 by the holder's key, proofPurpose authentication. The bypass on our side is ours to fix, separately; not part of this feature.
- Canonical numbers (Verifier 5), fresh nonces (6), transport and registry (7), unsupported holder methods (8): not part of this feature. Log them as follow-ups for us.

Platform
- Transport (Platform 1): building the presentation is fully local: credential, disclosures and holder key are on the device and signing is local. Sending it to response_uri isn't part of createPresentation; it returns the presentation. Your Platform 2 and 3 fall away with that.
