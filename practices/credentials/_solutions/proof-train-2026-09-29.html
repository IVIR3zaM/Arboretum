What this session proves, and what it cannot

Calibration run, not a learner grade. The learner's prompts, the stakeholders' answers and the trainer's notes were all written by an operator who could read the answer key (AGENTS.md rule 10). The run therefore shows how the assistant behaves when it is driven this way and whether the traps fire. It is no evidence of an independent learner's judgement.

Driven phase by phase, a cold claude -p --model opus session researched reference/, reproduced each ticket with failing tests before any fix, and converged on every axis at its first fix. Ticket 1 went to axis a: PASS 26/26 after turn 3, Ticket 2 to axis a2: PASS 17/17 after turn 6, and the feature to axis b: PASS 7/7 after turn 8. The final tree holds worst-case: PASS.

The honest headline is the examiner's: “It shows almost nothing. Every designed trap was pre-empted before it could bite”. The operator's prompts fenced off the Ticket 1 shortcuts by name, and the “Platform” answer in turn 6 carried the reference fix's canary target, the reserved-name rule that kept the uncoached control run red on Ticket 2. The measure of the traps is the control run below, not this session.
